# M45: GPU and RAM safeguards, measured

RTX 5080 Laptop (16 GB: 15.92 GiB as nvidia-smi counts it), 32 GB RAM (31.7), Windows 11, torch 2.8.0 cu128,
driver 617.42, 2026-10-10. Every run alone on the machine (the laptop froze and restarted on 2026-10-09/10 when
GPU jobs ran side by side or one of them overflowed the card).

## What the card really has

- Idle desktop: 0.47 GB used (nvidia-smi). A CUDA process's context + cuBLAS workspace: 0.27 GB, outside torch's
  accounting. torch sees 14.97 GB free of 15.89.
- The full refine Tile (M43b) reserved 15.57 GB, i.e. 15.57 + 0.47 + 0.27 > 15.92: Windows restarted (bugcheck
  0x20001). Hence the cap: what is free at the job's start − 0.3 GB (context) − 0.5 GB (margin) = 14.65 GB idle.

## The per-process cap works, with both allocators

| check | result |
|---|---|
| 0.8 GB cap, a 1.5 GB tensor (native allocator and cudaMallocAsync) | `OutOfMemoryError`, nothing spilled |
| trellis2 Fast with `EFFIGY_VRAM_BUDGET_GB=1` | exit 3 after 17 s, `ERROR oom "Allocation on device"` |
| Fast under the 14.6 GB cap / without | 81 s / 85 s, same result |
| Faithful under the cap, through the app | 101–111 s, step peak 9.2 GB, whole card 12.8 GB |

## Host RAM: what the generate tests found

Process peak (RSS of the step process) and the laptop's free RAM at its lowest, psutil every 0.5 s:

| run | process peak | free RAM min |
|---|---|---|
| Faithful, fresh worker | 19.3–21.7 GB | 4.9–5.0 GB |
| Fast, fresh process | 18.4 GB | 6.3 GB |
| Faithful → Fast swapped inside one worker | 21.3 GB | 2.9–3.4 GB |

In the swap the freed model's tensors go (13.4 → 0 GB) but the process keeps ~15 GB of heap, and Fast's 12.8 GB
of weights load on top. Through the app that swap failed twice: once the worker died after sampling, once it
crawled at 21 GB with the GPU idle (Windows paging). It was not the cap: the capped swap passed in a plain
process (free min 5.3 GB) and in the worker (3.4 GB) once the laptop had a little more room; uncapped it
passed with 6.2 GB. The margin was the whole difference.

So: `presets.RAM_GB["trellis2"] = 20`; a switch of `weights` evicts the resident worker and loads in a fresh
process; the pre-flight refuses a load while less than 20 GB of RAM is free ("close other programs"), with no
lower preset suggested (Fast needs it too). A 16 GB RAM machine cannot run trellis2 at all; Setup's RAM check
says so (RAM_LOW 15).

## Card layouts (tests/test_gpu_safeguards.py)

| card | Setup | refused | cap per job |
|---|---|---|---|
| RTX 4060 Laptop 8 GB | bad | every model | 6.8 GB |
| RTX 3060 12 GB | warn | Faithful (Fast suggested) | 10.4 GB |
| RTX 5080 Laptop 16 GB (reference) | ok | — | 14.7 GB |
| RTX 4080 16 GB, two 4K monitors + a browser (2.6 GB) | ok | Faithful "busy", Fast runs | 12.7 GB |
| RTX 4090 24 GB / RTX 5090 32 GB | ok | — | 22.6 / 30.6 GB |
| RTX A6000 48 GB, no display | ok | — | 47.2 GB |

Two GPUs: the one with the most memory is chosen and the steps are pinned to it (`CUDA_DEVICE_ORDER=PCI_BUS_ID`,
`CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES`); no NVIDIA GPU: no checks, the step reports its own error.
