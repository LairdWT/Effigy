## What the renders show

- **Pirate**: boots get laces and buckles, trouser cuffs a ruffle, the vest pockets and edging, hair at the back
  strands and braids. Hallucinations: white crease streaks on the back of the sleeves, a faint plaid on the back of
  the trousers, vertical seam lines on the vest's back, dark fingertips (painted nails) on both hands, a faint mark
  on the open chest. The **lite** Tile is noisier: blotchy light scuffs all over the vest, a messier back.
- **First run, face redrawn**: with the face views at `face_strength` 0.5 as planned, the face came back as another
  man (wide staring eyes, other features), worse than the projected one. The step now **keeps the texels `project`
  changed** (the face mask): the face is the input's, pixel for pixel; the views around it still draw hair and hat.
- **Farmer**: modest gain: more denim creases and fading, shoe detail, shirt folds. Losses: the jeans' back pockets
  fade, dark fingertips again, a patchy edge on the neck where the kept face mask meets redrawn skin.
- **Chest**: the lock goes from a blob to a readable hasp; the gold trim gets bevels and wear. The black panels get
  a slight green cast; the lid close-up shows an invented cross-hatch grid with gold scribbles. The chest is wider
  than one window, so its views are widened (extent 1.45 m) and the zoom is lower than planned.
- **Lantern**: the clearest win: the opaque white globe becomes believable clear glass with reflections; the brass
  gets dents and wear. Hallucination: tan wire wrapped round the glass. Drift 6.5 is mostly that white → clear glass.

## Metrics against the acceptance

- `drift` ≤ 4: holds for pirate, farmer and chest (2.3–3.2); the lantern (6.5) fails, by intent of the model
  (the glass).
- `detail` > 1.5×: **fails everywhere** (0.73–1.22). The band-pass energy (σ 1 − σ 4 px of luminance) counts the
  bake's speckle and voxel blotches as detail, the same trap as research 14's Laplacian variance: the renders show
  more structure, the number shows less noise. The metric needs replacing (or the owner's eye decides).
  **Replaced 2026-10-10** (code only, no bench run): `_band_energy` weights each pixel's band energy by the
  coherence of the gradients around it (the structure tensor's anisotropy, σ 2 px): on synthetic images white
  noise weighs about 0.1, blurred blotches about 0.3, stripes 1.0, and stripes score 24–30× noise of the same
  band energy (`tests/test_refine.py`, the step review's measurements). The 1.5× bar above was set for the old
  metric. To redo here: rerun the five assets and read `detail` again (`detail_metric: "coherence"` marks the new
  number).
- `seam_delta` no worse than the input's: **fails** (+0.4 to +3.0) although the step now levels seams after the
  views (M43a's `heal_seams`; without it the pirate went 4.24 -> 7.31). Two views paint the two sides of a UV seam
  from different pixels; the levelling fades over 2 texels only.

## Choices made in the step

- The face texels of a `project` chain are kept (not refined at 0.5); `face_strength` still applies to the views
  that see the face (their surroundings).
- `prompt` defaults to the brief's own words (`prompt`), not `prompt_full`: "full body, front view, soft key light"
  fights a zoomed back view.
- `torch.cuda.empty_cache()` before every view: the renders' blocks go back before the model runs (reserved
  16.73 -> 15.57 GB on the full Tile).
- A window wider than its square (T-pose arms, the chest) is widened, not tiled sideways: less zoom there.

## Left for the owner / M43c

- Full vs lite: full is cleaner but needs ~15.6 GB reserved on Windows; lite fits (12.2 GB) and is noisier. If lite
  ships, its file goes into the `refine` group (not yet in the manifest).
- Hallucinations need a per-view keep/drop (research 14 §6) or prompts that name the materials plainly.
- A real detail metric; seam handling across views (a wider levelling or blending views in the UV seams' 3D
  neighbourhood); the blind A/B in Godot.
