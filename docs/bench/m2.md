# M2 bench: generate (Fast = TRELLIS.2-4B, Faithful = Pixal3D)

RTX 5080 Laptop (16 GB, sm_120), driver 617.14, Windows 11, torch 2.8.0+cu128, 32 GB RAM. Input: the
`prep` cut-out of a Z-Image concept ("a dwarf blacksmith with hammer"), 1024². Preset `fast`:
`1024_cascade`, 12 steps per stage, 2048² textures, 200k faces. Times are the step's own metrics
(`seconds_load` = pipeline load in the resident worker, `seconds_sample` = `pipe.run`).

| Faithful, 10 golden images (Phase-0 bench) | median 160 (106–204) | – | – | 13.3 GB | – | 10/10 ok, closer to the image than Fast on 7/10 (`phase0.md`) |

- Peak VRAM (torch): Fast 3.0–7.6 GB, Faithful 13.3 GB, both under the 14.5 GB budget. The whole-GPU
  overshoot seen in the bench (mesh decoding fragmenting torch's allocator) is fixed with
  `cudaMallocAsync` (`phase0.md`).
- The second run's sampling is ~half the first: FlexGEMM's one-time Triton autotune is cached in
  `models/flex_gemm_autotune_cache.json` after the first run.
- Load: ~11.5 s of every 1.3B model's load was upstream constructing it in fp32 with random init
  before `load_state_dict` (reading the weights takes < 1 s). Patch 13 (`skip_random_init`) skips the
  init: the same checkpoint now loads in 1.3 s, all 640 tensors identical. The runs above predate it.
- Upstream's "DC remesh fragmentation" check doesn't apply: generate uses upstream's non-remesh branch
  (CuMesh simplify + unwrap).

## Game-ready chain on `060_trellis2` (M3, M4, M7, M13)

`clean {}` → `remesh {"faces": 20000}` → `uv {}` → `bake {"texture_size": 2048}`, then `validate` and
`export` godot / unreal on the bake. Each step is its own process.
