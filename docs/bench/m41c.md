# M41c: the dragon

`python -m tools.creature_bench fox horse bird kaiju shark dragon --glb <cat>=fox --glb <test kaiju>=kaiju --glb
<test shark>=shark --glb <test dragon>=dragon`, as in `m41b.md` (the `test` rows are copies of the owner's `test kaiju`,
`test shark`, `test dragon` `005_clean`). Before = M41b (9d6bdf2, its "After" table), after = M41c. Inside is after
rig_fit's snap, score is Check fit's.

What changed (`steps/_templates.py`):

- **A limb chain runs on through its longest child** when that child reaches half as far again as the others: the
  wing chain is `shoulder → wing_1 … wing_5 → wing_tip` (before it stopped at the shoulder, two children), the five
  membrane fingers hang off it. Fingers of a hand (about as long as each other) still end a chain.
- **What hangs off a traced chain turns and stretches with the whole chain** (its root to its tip), not per bone: the
  template's wing bones zigzag (the folded arm), a turn per bone swung the fingers inward.
- **A template wider than long** (`wide()`: dragon, bird, spider): voxels as many as a cube 64 wide (the flat sample
  dragon got a body 4 voxels deep), the axis from the thick core seen from above (`_core`: deeper than half the
  greatest depth; its horizontal PCA when 1.5:1 or longer, else the proportions as before: a dragon with folded wings
  has a long box, the template a wide one), and the centre line also traced through a band as wide as that core (the
  wings' box put the body's sides into the band: no slice had a top and a bottom, the trunk stayed on the box's
  guess). Of the two lines, the one putting more of the trunk in the mesh after the snap (the sample lying flat: the
  core's; the test dragon sitting up: the box's).
- **A foot lies on the template's floor** (the leaf bone's head and tail within a tenth of the height): the dragon's
  wing tip only touches it and is no longer a leg that may go down to the floor. A leg takes only a thin part that
  reaches below its root (a level wing is not a leg), and the chains off the floor go first (the sitting test dragon's
  wings hang lower than its chest).
