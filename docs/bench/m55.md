# M55 bench: parallax layers from one picture (2026-10-13)

RTX 5080 Laptop (16 GB), Windows 11, torch 2.8.0+cu128. The input was a 2048 × 768 street background made by
`concepts` op `image` (`background`, "a run-down city street at dusk…"), seed 5.

## The depth model

The owner asked for "the best quality model that fits our commercial usability requirement". The licences were read
on Hugging Face on 2026-10-13.

| Model | Licence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Depth Anything 3 Giant / Large, DA3 Nested | CC-BY-NC-4.0 | out |
| Depth Anything 3 Mono Large | Apache-2.0 | Allowed, but its package needs xformers (not on sm_120 here), open3d, pycolmap and `numpy<2`: a vendoring job |
| Depth Anything V2 Small | Apache-2.0 | Allowed, clearly weaker (Base and Large are NC) |
| Depth Pro (Apple) | apple-amlr | out |
| VGGT-1B | CC-BY-NC-4.0 | out |
| Marigold depth v1-1 | OpenRAIL++ | Allowed, but slow (diffusion) |
| MoGe-2 ViT-L | MIT | Allowed, already on disk (Faithful's group) |
| **MoGe-3 ViT-g** | **MIT** (code and helpers MIT) | **chosen**: the newest and largest; MoGe's fine-detail refinement |

The spike, on the street picture, one process for both:

| | load s | infer s | torch GB | no depth (sky) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MoGe-2 ViT-L | 2.4 | 8.9 | 2.84 | 11.1 % |
| MoGe-3 ViT-g | 7.8 | 11.0 | 8.93 | 11.2 % |

Cut into four equal-share bands, MoGe-2's left building had specks of the nearest band in its upper windows and
patches on the right facade. MoGe-3 kept those surfaces whole.

MoGe-3 imports `flex_gemm.nn`, which only FlexGEMM newer than our TRELLIS.2 wheel has (upstream b2fadb2, pure
Triton). So it gets its own `.venv-moge`; `.venv-ml` is unchanged.

## The steps through the app

| Step | s | torch GB | card GB | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `depth` | 19.3 (16.1 in the step) | 8.93 | 10.06 | 11.2 % without depth (the sky) |
| `layers` (4) | 103.1 (99.0) | 7.70 | 9.91 | speeds 0.05 / 0.281 / 0.536 / 1, shares 11 / 30 / 51 / 8 % |
| `validate` | 0.6 | – | – | 0 fails (`layers` 4, `far_layer_whole` 0) |
| `export` godot-2d | 2.3 | – | – | `parallax.tscn` + four layers |

About 26 s of the layers step is Z-Image's load. It draws three layers at 2048 × 768 with the VAE tiled. Neither step
has a `RAM_GB` row: Z-Image in a fresh process stays well under the 32 GB laptop's limit, as `concepts` does.

## What changed on the way

- **Bands**: equal shares cut the receding road and the angled facades into stripes. Now k-means on the
  disparity, so the cuts fall in its gaps. The sky (no depth) is always the farthest band. A speck under 0.2 % of the
  picture joins the band round it, 8-connected.
- **What the nearer layers hide**: at strength 1 with "the empty scenery behind, nothing in front", Z-Image drew a
  photographic street behind a painted one, and a ghost of the lamp post in the sky layer. Now OpenCV fills each
  layer's hidden part from its own colours, and Z-Image redraws that fill with the brief's `prompt_full`, which
  carries the kind's look.
  - At 0.6 the far layer came out as a hazy perspective street, and the drawn-in facades as dark smudges.
  - At **0.8**: a hazy side-on row of shops (atmospheric distance), and shuttered facades behind the near buildings.

## The owner's check: a car cut in two, then its tyres

- **The car in two halves** (`004_layers`).
  - The car is parked along the street, so its roof is farther than its bumper: disparity 0.155 to 0.176, across
    the cut at 0.165.
  - The roof went to layer 1 and the bumper to layer 2. Layer 1 then drew a ghost of the car from the roof it held.
- **The fix: the cuts follow the depth's edges.** Pixels within 35 % of the gap of a cut are decided by a watershed
  (`scipy.ndimage.watershed_ift` on the disparity's gradient) from the sure ones.
  - It runs through smooth depth (a car's body, the road under it) and stops at sharp jumps (the car's outline
    against the far buildings).
  - The car comes out whole in one layer (`007_layers`); the lamp post and plants whole in the next. 0.1 s.
  - A test builds the leaning car: by depth alone it falls in two, with the watershed it is whole.
- **Its tyres then went with the road.** Under the tyres the depth is the road's, so there is no edge to follow.
  - Growing the farther band down into the ground it stands on (3 % of the height) took the tyres back.
  - But it also took parts of the plants standing against the shop's wall, where there is no depth step either. With
    a guard at depth edges it was the tyres or the plants. Not shipped.
  - SAM 2.1 Large (Apache-2.0, through transformers 5.18's mask-generation, 3.65 GB, 3–14 s) found 36–59 objects in
    the painted picture, but not the car, at any threshold tried.
  - Keeping a whole object together needs object masks prompted where a cut runs (SAM at the cut, or SAM 3 with the
    description's nouns). A later item.
- **Ghost cars.** The repaint's prompt is the description, and that lists "a parked car". What the nearer road hid
  in layer 1 came back as two more cars; with only the kind's look, still cars.
  - With ", empty ground, nothing standing on it" after the description, the hidden road is empty road
    (`008_layers`).
  - Left: a faint haze in the farthest layer's sky where the lamp's glow was filled in.
- **"The animated loop is not the different layer images."** The loop is the four files stacked. Each nearer layer
  covers most of the ones behind, so the stack looks like the picture, and what the farther ones hold shows only as
  the camera moves.
  - Opened alone, the farthest layer is mostly a redrawn hazy street.
  - The view now has a Show menu: all layers scrolling, each layer alone, or the bands.

## Godot 4.7.1

The `godot-2d` export in a scratch project, headless: `parallax.tscn` loads with four `Parallax2D`. A `Camera2D`
moved 1000 px moves the layers on screen by 50.0 / 281.0 / 536.0 / 1000.0 px, exactly their `scroll_scale`.
