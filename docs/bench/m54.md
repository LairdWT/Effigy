### What changed on the way

- **`from_pipe` cast the DiT to float32.** That is its default `dtype`: 15.45 GB, then an OOM. The inpaint pipeline
  is now built from `pipe.components`, as they are. The cast also changed the shared module in the resident worker,
  so a worker that has run it must be stopped.
- **Mend 1.0 draws the band afresh.**
  - It comes out darker or lighter, and in a cobbled floor with larger stones: a frame round every tile when
    repeated.
  - Now: 0.8 (0.6 looked the same), and the band's colours matched to what the picture had there.
- **The tile prompt drew perspective.**
  - With "flat straight-on view", the cobbles shrank towards the top.
  - "A flat lay seen straight from above, uniform scale everywhere with no perspective" fixed it.
  - "Large simple shapes, only a few across" did not shrink a brick wall's count. With the pixel style, the bricks
    stayed small even when the description asked for six across; without the style, about ten across.
- **The seam measure.**
  - Against the 90th percentile of the tile's own lines, every brick and cobble tile looked seamed: their mortar
    lines reach 1.3–1.9 times it.
  - Against its strongest line, a cut-out piece scores 3.6, a seamless synthetic texture 0.87, and every mended
    tile 0.58–1.02.
- **Pixel art from a tile.**
  - At 32 px a 512 tile's ten bricks across are 3 px each, and melt.
  - At 64 px with the most frequent colour, the texture's noise wins. With the middle colour they are clean bricks
    that repeat with no seam.
  - The form now starts a tile at an eighth of its size, with the middle colour unless the tile was drawn
    pixel-style.
