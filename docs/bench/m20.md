(the learned engine and the engine choice follow below).

## What it took

1. **Vertices the proxy gave nothing** (pieces whose proxy shell bone heat couldn't solve: 4-46 per
   character): they take the weights of the nearest weighted vertex.
2. **Pose stretch** over all edges was dominated by a raw mesh's micro-edges (126×); edges under 0.5 %
   of the height are left out.
3. **Fused limbs**: where a hand touches the body the proxy fuses them and bone heat spread the goblin's
   pinky over its coat (24×). Hand and finger bones keep weights only on mesh connected to them (a held
   prop like the dwarf's hammer is, and keeps its weights); for every bone that rule cut legitimate
   weights on the robot built from pieces (pose volume 8 → 54 %), so it's hands only.
4. **The robot's spine sat in its chest plate**: rig_fit put torso joints in the middle of the first
   surface layer from the front, 19 cm forward of the body's centre, and the chest followed the left arm
   (10× stretch). Torso joints now sit between the front-most and back-most surface (10 → 2.3×); a joint
   in a hollow torso counts as inside when the surface encloses it along all three axes.

UI: "Skin it" in the Rig panel, the QA list with what each check means, the viewer's "Weights: <bone>"
heat map (blue 0 → red 1) and a Skeleton toggle.

## SkinTokens (engine `skintokens`), measured 2026-10-05

The MIT no-Blender port of SkinTokens (pinned commit, `pyproject` group `skin`) in its own `.venv-skin`
(torch 2.8 cu128, transformers 5, no flash-attn: it loads on the SDPA fallback); weights group `skin`
(TokenRig + skin VAE 1.5 GB, Qwen3-0.6B config only) under `models/SkinTokens`. Skin-only mode on our
52 joints, which the model knows by their `mixamorig:` names (part-order table); the output's joints are
the tokenizer's quantized copies, so our exact skeleton and names go back on before the rig is
transferred onto the textured input GLB.

| | |
|---|---|
| **peak VRAM** | **3.43 GB** on every character (the README's "≥ 14 GB" is the author's server, not the model; research 05 expected ~4) |
| time | 27–37 s per character on the raw 180–215k-vertex meshes, 20 s of it loading the model |
| class key | `mixamo` (part tokens, no class token): farmer all pass but stretch 5.6× warn; `articulation` (class token, no part tokens): stretch 31× fail. The checkpoint's class tokens are rignet, vroid, articulation; `mixamo` exists as a part-order table only |

Engine `auto` (the default): both engines, judged by `blender_ops/rig_qa` (§3.9), fewest fails, then
warnings, then the smallest stretch. The other engine's file stays beside the pick as `rigged_<engine>.glb`.

worst edge's stretch still moves a little between runs (65× vs 61× on the farmer: CUDA), never the pick.
