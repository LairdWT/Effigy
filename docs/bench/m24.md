# M24: faces, round two (2026-10-06)

**Accepted by the owner on 2026-10-06** ("good for now").

After M23 (`m23.md`) two limits remained: the face is about 150 px tall in the conditioning image, and
the image is lit while the baked albedo is not. Both measured on the nurse's head, then run on eight
characters (`m24_faces.jpg`: image | bake | M24 projection).

## Upscale: Real-ESRGAN x4plus

| | |
|---|---|
| image | 986² → 3944² |
| time | 2.0 s |
| VRAM peak | 0.96 GB |
| eyes, lips, hair edges | visibly sharper than bicubic 4× (`scratchpad` sheet reviewed by eye) |

Weights: the v0.1.0 release file, BSD-3-Clause, sha256 `4fa0d389…`; model group `texture`. Licence
checked 2026-10-06: the repository LICENSE (BSD-3-Clause) covers the released weights, trained on DF2K
(DIV2K + Flickr2K) and OST, public research sets; no separate weight licence, no non-commercial term. The network is
written out in `steps/_sr.py` (23 RRDB blocks, 64 features, growth 32) and loads the file by its own key
names, so neither basicsr nor the realesrgan package is needed (both have a history of breaking with new
torch releases).

## Delighting: Marigold-IID tried, dropped

`prs-eth/marigold-iid-appearance-v1-1` (OpenRAIL++: allowed but flagged by PLAN §6), 2.58 GB, loaded in
6 min (download), 12.9 s on a 275² head crop, 4.05 GB VRAM peak. Its albedo removed the shading and the
detail with it: the nurse's eyes and lips became flat shapes, the skin one tone, a painting. For a game
face that is the wrong trade. Not adopted; the weights were deleted.

What replaced it, with no model: a **local colour match**. The bake as the camera sees it (every z-buffer
pixel's surface point looked up in the atlas) and the image are both blurred over about a nose's length
(σ = 8 % of the figure's height; at 1/8 resolution, masks carried along), and the per-pixel difference is
added to the projected image. The image's large-scale shading (the nose shadow, the cheek highlight
gradient) becomes the bake's flat albedo; the image's detail stays. M23's global mean shift stays
available as `match: "mean"`.

## Open

- One view only; the sides and back of the head keep the bake. A second image (the turnaround's back
  view) could feed the same step.
- The local match removes large-scale shading only; a sharp shadow edge (under a cap's brim) stays.
- The upscaler is a general photo model; an anime-tuned one (`x4plus_anime_6B`, same licence) might
  suit the cartoon characters better. Unmeasured.
