# M16 bench: pose canonicalization (re-pose a character to an A-pose before 3D)

RTX 5080 Laptop (16 GB), driver 617.14, max power, Windows 11, torch 2.8.0+cu128, diffusers 0.40.0,
32 GB RAM. 2026-10-04.

**Result: FLUX.2-klein-4B, 9/10 pass (acceptance ≥ 7/10 met); Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 + AnyPose dropped.**

## Inputs

- 10 characters in poses that break rigging (`tests/fixtures/characters/`, our own prompts through
  Z-Image with the new `posed` brief category): knight raising a sword, archer drawing a bow (side-on),
  mage with staff and spell hand, cartoon runner mid-stride, robot with crossed arms, pirate with a foot
  on a barrel, chef holding a pan, crouching goblin with a dagger, dancer on one leg arms up, farmer
  leaning on a pitchfork. Gate before re-pose: 8 `other`, 2 `unsure` (side-on archer, flat 2D runner).
- Reference (image 2): `prompts/apose_ref.png`. Z-Image can't be prompted into an A-pose (8/8
  mannequin candidates: arms 10–18°) and DWPose can't read a primitive stick figure's arms (wrist score
  0.07), so the reference is a Z-Image mannequin re-posed by FLUX.2-klein onto a Blender stick-figure
  guide (`blender_ops/apose_guide.py`): 4/4 seeds gated `a`, arms 41–44°.
- Gate: DWPose (CPU onnxruntime, person box from the cut-out) + arm/elbow angles, `steps/_pose.py`.

- **Prompt matters:** with AnyPose's trigger prompt (written for Qwen) klein kept the knight's raised
  sword arms and added the mannequin's (four arms). The klein prompt in `steps/canon.py` says what to
  keep (design, clothes, colours, proportions) and what to drop (held objects, the mannequin's look,
  extra arms); with it the knight came out right on seeds 1–2.
- **The gate has two blind spots:** the knight at seed 7 still had extra raised arms holding the sword,
  but DWPose followed the A-pose pair and passed it (false pass); the runner is clearly in an A-pose but
  measured 64–65°, just above the 60° band (false fail). Counting limbs needs a second detector or a
  check that the cut-out's silhouette has no limbs above the shoulders; not built.
- **Qwen:** loading worked (GGUF transformer + LoRAs), but with model CPU offload the 13.2 GB Q4
  transformer plus activations filled the card; after 11 min no step had finished, so it was cancelled.
  A smaller quant (Q3_K_M, 9.9 GB) or block-level group offload might fit, but klein already passes
  9/10 at 27 s. Its weights (~31 GB under `models/Qwen-Image-Edit-2511*`, `models/AnyPose`) are no longer
  used; delete them by hand if the space is needed.
