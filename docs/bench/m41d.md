# M41d: the spider

`python -m tools.creature_bench fox horse bird kaiju shark dragon spider --glb <cat>=fox --glb <test kaiju>=kaiju --glb
<test shark>=shark --glb <test dragon>=dragon --glb <test spider>=spider`, as in `m41c.md` (the `test` rows are copies
of the owner's `test <id>` `005_clean`). Before = M41c (37ed52c), after = M41d. Inside is after rig_fit's snap, score is
Check fit's. The bench counts 55 spider joints: its root on the floor is left out (rig_fit counts it: 56).

What changed (`steps/_templates.py`):

- **A hub starts a chain per child**: the spider's `legs_anchor_<s>` has four legs of about the same reach and is
  shorter than them, so each leg (`legs_anchor_<k>_<s>` → `leg_<x>_1 … tip`) is its own chain (before: one chain that
  stopped at the anchor). A hand's fingers, shorter than the arm before them, still end the arm's chain.
- **A thin part standing on the floor in several feet splits per foot** (`_thin`): the legs on a side meet in front of
  the abdomen and the opening leaves them one part; each voxel goes to the foot it is nearest along the part.
- **A wide template's middle line onto the solid's middle** along the body: abdomen tip to fangs onto where the mesh's
  middle plane is solid, not its box onto the legs' (the spider's front legs reach further forward than its back legs
  back: the head was put in front of the mesh's).
- **`HEAD_LOW = {"spider"}`**: the head at the end with less body above the back.
- **A wide template's flat mesh stood up** (`level`): the test spider lies tilted 37° (generated from a picture taken
  from above). Its floor is the convex hull's face within 30° of down along its flattest axis that most of the mesh
  touches (the axis alone leans towards a high abdomen; the largest face would be one from a dragon's feet to its wing
  tip); turned flat when 3°-45° off. It leaves every other bench mesh as it is (wide templates only: the bird and
dragon samples and the test dragon unchanged in the table).
- **Legs spread all round take the core's lean**: when the whole footprint gives no lean (roundish), a wide template's
  thick core gives it, from 10° (the test spider: 43°; a box dragon's neck to one side gave 3.7°).
