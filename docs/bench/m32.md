# M32: rigid parts on the skeleton (2026-10-08)

A part marked rigid (a helmet, a shield, a weapon) gets all its weight on one bone, by default the body
bone passing nearest its centre (`steps/_rig.nearest_bone`); the other parts bend as before. Run on the
reference laptop through the app's API, on the viking (`library/bench_faithful_viking`):

| version | step | result |
|---|---|---|
| `527_parts` | parts `segment` on `523_parts` (3 clicks on the helmet, 1 on the face, front view) | Helmet 1 083 faces (the dome and the ears), beside Torso and Arms, Legs, Skirt, Belt, Body; 35 s, peak VRAM 1.75 GB |
| `528_rig_fit` | rig_fit (detected) | 52/52 joints inside; `parts.json` carried along |
| `529_rig_skin` | rig_skin `{"rigid": {"Helmet": ""}}` | Helmet → **Head** (the default); bone heat picked, 0 fails, stretch 2.974 (the unsplit bake in M31: 2.981), volume change 3.96 %; SkinTokens stretch 23.995, 38 s, 3.39 GB |
| `530_anim` | anim Walk + `500_motion` ("angrily hide behind a wall") | six skinned objects named like the parts, two clips |
| `531_export`, `532_export` | export Godot, Unreal | below |

## Rigid, measured

Every frame of both clips, the helmet's vertices against the rest pose carried by the Head bone's pose
(Blender, the clip picked by assigning its action; the scratch script `rigidcheck.py` pattern):

| clip | frames | helmet, rigid (`530_anim`) | helmet, bending (the same rig skinned without `rigid`, scratch) | Body within 15 cm of the Head joint (control) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `500_motion` | 121 | **0.00000 m** max | 0.01623 m max, 0.0124 m p95 | 0.15058 m |
| Walk | 41 | **0.00000 m** max | 0.00254 m max, 0.00181 m p95 | 0.02335 m |

Bone heat already gave the helmet mostly to Head, so the bending is small (1.6 cm at most, in the
custom motion's head turns); rigid it is exactly zero. `m32_helmet.jpg`: the rigid run (left) and the
bending one (right), three frames each of `500_motion` (rows 1–2: body, head) and Walk (rows 3–4): the
helmet sits on the head in every frame, no gap, nothing through it.

## Engines

| engine | result |
|---|---|
| Godot 4.7.1 (headless import, scene walked) | one `Skeleton3D` (52 bones) with six skinned `MeshInstance3D` named like the parts; **Helmet uses one bone, Head**; the others 8–46 bones; `AnimationPlayer` with `500_motion` and Walk; a `StaticBody3D` hull per part (in the rest pose, as rigged exports had since M21) |
| Unreal 5.8 (headless import, default) | the six skinned parts **combined into one skeletal mesh named after the first part (`Belt`)**, its skeleton and physics asset, the two clips, the textures; the six `UCX_` hulls arrive as loose static meshes (a skeletal mesh uses its physics asset; rigged exports carried the hull since M21, never imported into Unreal before) |
| Unreal 5.8, Interchange "Combine Skeletal Meshes" = Do Not Combine | six skeletal meshes Belt, Body, Helmet, Legs, Skirt, Torso_and_Arms on **one** skeleton, the two clips |

Rigged exports' collision hulls (static, rest pose) are no use on an animated character in either engine:
the export panel's Collision hull tickbox now defaults to off for rigged and animated versions (owner
2026-10-08: a tickbox, the user decides); static models keep it on.
